SECTION 2 - LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE POLICY
============================================================

Employees should submit planned leave requests through the
company's HR portal.

Leave requests should be submitted to the reporting manager
for approval. Employees should notify their manager if an
unexpected absence prevents them from attending work.

Attendance records are maintained by the HR department.
Employees who notice an attendance discrepancy should raise
a request with HR.

For questions about leave balances or attendance corrections,
employees should contact the HR operations team.

SECTION 3 - EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING
============================================================

New employees must complete the onboarding checklist.

The checklist includes:

1. Completing required employment documentation.
2. Reviewing the employee handbook.
3. Receiving an assigned company workstation.
4. Completing account setup with IT Support.
5. Enabling multi-factor authentication where supported.
6. Attending the company security awareness session.

Managers should confirm that onboarding tasks are completed
before granting access to business applications.

============================================================
SECTION 4 - IT SUPPORT AND ACCOUNT SECURITY
============================================================

Employees should report technical problems through the
internal IT helpdesk.

IT Support handles:

- Workstation and laptop troubleshooting.
- Business application access issues.
- Network connectivity problems.
- Approved software installation requests.
- Employee account provisioning and recovery.

Employees must not share their passwords or authentication
codes with colleagues or support personnel.

Multi-factor authentication should remain enabled on supported
business accounts.

Suspected phishing emails, unexpected login notifications,
and lost company devices should be reported to IT Support
promptly.

============================================================
SECTION 5 - PASSWORD AND MFA POLICY
============================================================

Employees are responsible for protecting their account
credentials.

Passwords must not be written in publicly accessible locations
or shared through ordinary chat messages.

Employees should use the approved password reset process
when they believe their credentials may be compromised.

MFA verification requests should only be approved when the
employee initiated the corresponding login.
